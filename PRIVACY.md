# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-08-05

US History Timeline is a free, independent study game. This policy explains, in plain
terms, what the site does and does not do with information.

## The short version

Everything you do in the game stays in your own browser. There are no accounts.
We never ask for your name, email address, age, or school. We cannot see your
study notes.

## What is stored on your device

The game saves the following in your browser's local storage. This never leaves
your device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else:

| Key | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `apush-game-state` | Your current game: timeline, score, attempts, streak |
| `apush-settings` | Your preferences: dark mode, hard mode, filters, analytics choice |
| `apush-notes` | Study notes you write yourself |
| `apush-event-count` | Internal bookkeeping for the event list |
| `apush-data-version` | Internal bookkeeping so old saves upgrade cleanly |

To erase all of it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

**On a shared or school computer**, someone using the browser after you could
open the app and read notes you left behind. Clear the site data if that matters
to you.

## Anonymous usage statistics

The site uses Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to count page views
and measure loading performance.

- They set **no cookies**.
- They do **not** identify you and cannot follow you to other websites.
- Visitors are counted using a hash derived from the incoming request, which is
  **discarded and reset every 24 hours**.
- The scripts are served from this site's own domain, not a third-party host.

Details from the provider: [Web Analytics privacy](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy)
and [Speed Insights privacy](https://vercel.com/docs/speed-insights/privacy-policy).

**You can turn this off.** Open **About & privacy** at the bottom of the page and
choose "Turn analytics off". The choice is saved in your browser and stops the
analytics scripts from loading at all.

## Third parties

The site loads no third-party scripts, fonts, images, or trackers. Fonts are
served from this site's own domain specifically so that visiting the page does
not disclose your IP address to anyone else.

The site is hosted by [Vercel](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy), which
processes standard server request data in order to serve the page.

## Children

This is a study tool for an Advanced Placement course, whose students are
typically 15 to 17 years old. It is not directed to children under 13, contains
no advertising, and collects no personal information from anyone. Because the app
has no accounts and never asks for a date of birth, it has no way of knowing a
visitor's age.

## Changes

Any material change to this policy will be reflected in the "last updated" date
above.
